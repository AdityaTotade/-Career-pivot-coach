# Demo video script: Career Pivot Coach (about 2 min 30 s)

Record the live site, https://career-pivot-coach.vercel.app, in a clean browser window (or press **Start over** first). Keep Wispr Flow running. Speak slowly; cut pauses in editing.

| Time | On screen | Say |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:15 | Hero page | "Switching careers usually starts with a guess about what the new role needs. Career Pivot Coach replaces that guess with real job postings: 336 of them, across 22 roles." |
| 0:15–0:25 | Scroll to the 7-step strip | "It covers the whole brief: intake, a skill-gap map, a weekly plan, projects, mock interviews, progress tracking and an application tracker." |
| 0:25–0:50 | Click **Start**, click the experience box, hold the **Wispr Flow** key and speak 2–3 lines of a real background (e.g. operations executive, Excel reports, SOPs) | "I'll just speak my experience with Wispr Flow, no typing." *(let the text appear)* "Then I pick the role I want: Business Analyst." |
| 0:50–1:15 | Click **Build my plan** → verdict card counts up | "In about a second I get my match against nineteen real Business Analyst postings, the skills I already show, and the three to close first. Every gap links to the companies that asked for it." *(hover a company link)* |
| 1:15–1:30 | Click the **Data Analyst** chip; the plan rebuilds | "Change the role and the whole plan rebuilds: different market, different gaps." |
| 1:30–1:50 | **Plan** tab: week 1 tasks, click **Mark done** | "One main gap a week, sized to my hours, with a free resource and a proof task. If I slip, the week repeats." |
| 1:50–2:15 | **Practice**: dictate an answer with Wispr Flow → **Score my answer** | "Interview practice for this week's skill. I answer out loud." *(score appears)* "It scores structure, specifics, relevance, ownership and length, and tells me how to raise it. Under 60 adds a review week to my plan." |
| 2:15–2:25 | **Applications** section | "And as I log applications, it learns which kinds of roles actually reply." |
| 2:25–2:35 | Footer | "AI interprets, rules validate, you approve. Every number here is counted, not guessed. Career Pivot Coach, by Team Gorkha." |

**Checklist before recording:** Start over is clicked · the sample person is not used (speak your own lines) · browser zoom at 100% · notifications off.
